About Queen's Banquet

Queen's Banquet is a banquet hall on the ground floor of B1, Block B, 49 Canto Road, Sector 17A Colony, Lucknow. We built it as one spacious room that you can fill with your own idea of a celebration.

The hall

The ceiling is lit with soft cove lights and three crystal chandeliers. The floor is polished marble, and the buffet runs along the walls so the middle stays open for guests, dancing and stage setup.

Outside, green walls, plants and a fountain make the entrance a photo spot of its own.

The food

Our catering is in-house. Dishes are made fresh for your event and served from the buffet counters. Menus are planned with you, and you can ask us for tasting details when you call.
